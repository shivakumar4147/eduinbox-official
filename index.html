About the App

EduInbox helps students stay organized by automatically identifying academic attachments in their emails and categorizing them into local folders. No more searching through hundreds of emails for that one lecture slide.

Key Features:

  • Automatic attachment detection.
  • Local folder synchronization.
  • Professor-specific categorization.
