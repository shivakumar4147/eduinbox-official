About EduInbox

EduInbox is a productivity tool designed for university students. Our app securely connects to your email to scan for academic materials like PDFs, PPTs, and assignments, automatically organizing them into dedicated folders on your device.

Core Features:

  • Automated Sync: Instantly fetches new materials from linked professor emails.
  • Smart Categorization: Sorts files by subject and instructor automatically.
  • Offline Access: View your lecture notes and assignments anytime without an internet connection.
  • Eisenhower Matrix: Built-in task manager to prioritize your academic workload.

Security & Transparency

EduInbox uses the Gmail API in read-only mode. We do not delete, compose, or send emails. All processed files are stored locally on your device for maximum privacy.
